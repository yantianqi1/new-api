---
title: Groups
---

A token's group decides which pool of upstream channels serves your requests and which price multiplier applies.

### What groups are

Each group maps to a set of upstream channels plus a price multiplier. To compare a model's price across groups, open its details on the Pricing page.

There are two modes:

| Mode | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Follow account group (default) | The token has no group of its own and always follows your account's current group |
| A specific group | Locked to that group; requests fail if it becomes unavailable |
